Our story

Luxury that does not ask for much

Draft copy

Shafar began with one stubborn idea: a perfume should smell expensive without being expensive.

Every fragrance carries the name of a place. Halila Lane, Belford, Charleston, Shawano. A name on the label and a story in the bottle.

We bottle in Parfum and Eau de Parfum strength, in 50 ml bottles, and deliver across Pakistan with cash on delivery.

Still needed from you: the year Shafar started, and the city. That becomes the opening line of this story.

The making

How a bottle is made

Draft steps
  1. 01

    Sourcing

    It starts with the oils. Each one is chosen for how it smells on skin hours later, not in the first minute.

  2. 02

    Blending

    The notes are blended in small batches and adjusted until the balance is right.

  3. 03

    Maturing

    The blend is left to rest, so the separate notes settle into one scent.

  4. 04

    Bottling

    Filled into 50 ml bottles, then capped and labelled.

  5. 05

    Checking

    Every bottle is checked before it is boxed and sent to you.
